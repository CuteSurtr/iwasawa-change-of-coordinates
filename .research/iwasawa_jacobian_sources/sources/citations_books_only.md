# Citation-only references (no legal free PDF available)

These books are paywalled. Bibliographic info only; do NOT attempt to
download pirated copies.

## Helgason, S. (2001)
*Differential Geometry, Lie Groups, and Symmetric Spaces.*
American Mathematical Society, Graduate Studies in Mathematics, vol 34.
ISBN: 978-0-8218-2848-9.
Originally Academic Press, 1978.

Relevant material:
- Chapter VI sections 3-5: Iwasawa decomposition for noncompact
  semisimple Lie groups.
- Chapter I section 5: integration on Lie groups (left/right Haar,
  modular function).
- SL(n, R)/SO(n, R) example: appears throughout, especially in Chapter
  IX (symmetric spaces of the noncompact type).
- Notation: Helgason often uses ρ for the FULL sum of positive roots,
  whereas Knapp uses 2ρ for that sum. Convert accordingly.

## Muirhead, R. J. (1982)
*Aspects of Multivariate Statistical Theory.*
Wiley series in Probability and Mathematical Statistics.
Wiley, 1982 (reprinted 2005 in paperback).
ISBN: 978-0-471-09442-2.

Relevant material:
- Theorem 2.1.13 and 2.1.14: Jacobian of the QR / triangular
  decomposition for matrix-variate statistics.
- Same exponent n - i (in standard upper-R convention) as Edelman-Rao.

## Bump, D. (1997)
*Automorphic Forms and Representations.*
Cambridge Studies in Advanced Mathematics, vol 55.
Cambridge University Press, 1997.
ISBN: 978-0-521-65818-7.

Partial preview on Google Books.

Relevant material:
- Section 2.3: Iwasawa decomposition for GL(2, R).
- Later chapters: extension to GL(n) and adelic versions.

## Diaz-Garcia, J. A.; Gutierrez-Jaimez, R. (various)
Several arXiv papers on matrix-variate Jacobians (QR, polar, SVD,
Cholesky). Search arXiv for the author pair "Diaz-Garcia QR" or
"Diaz-Garcia decomposition Jacobian"; most are legally available.
Useful for cross-checking conventions in matrix-variate statistics
literature.

## Mathai, A. M. (1997)
*Jacobians of Matrix Transformations and Functions of Matrix Argument.*
World Scientific.
ISBN: 978-981-02-2887-9.

Cited by Edelman-Rao for the QR Jacobian formula.

## Olkin, I. (1953)
"Note on 'the Jacobians of certain matrix transformations useful in
multivariate analysis'." Biometrika 40, 43-46.

Original source for the QR Jacobian power formula. Cited by Edelman
handouts.

## Knapp first edition

Knapp, A. W. (2002).
*Lie Groups Beyond an Introduction.*
Second edition. Birkhauser. Progress in Mathematics, vol 140.
ISBN: 978-0-8176-4259-4.

The first-printed second edition is paywalled. The "Digital Second
Edition" (~2023) is freely available on the author's site at
<https://www.math.stonybrook.edu/~aknapp/download/Beyond2.pdf>, with
the author's blessing. Use the digital edition for citations.
